Up to 50 MB/s Transfer Speed

100% Zero-Cloud Architecture

Your personal photos and sensitive documents never travel through any intermediate proxy or remote cloud database. They stay exclusively between the two devices.

Simple 3-Step Flow

Sharing Files Is Effortless

Connect and transfer files in three simple, intuitive steps.

1

Select Your Files

Tap Send on Dropify and select photos, videos, apps, or documents using the native Android file picker. Select as many as you need.

2

Scan QR or Tap Radar

Have the recipient tap Receive. Their device appears immediately on your radar, or you can scan their QR code to pair in under 1 second.

3

Accept & Enjoy

The recipient taps Accept. Files stream at up to 50 MB/s directly into their Downloads/Dropify folder. No data used, no quality lost!

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Dropify's security, technology, and downloads.

Is Dropify really 100% free with no hidden charges? ▼
Yes, completely free! Dropify is created by independent developer Subhojit Haldar as a fast, clean, private nearby sharing utility. There are zero subscriptions, zero locked features, and zero in-app ads.
Do both phones need to have Dropify installed? ▼
Yes, both sender and receiver must have Dropify installed to establish the encrypted Wi-Fi Direct peer-to-peer connection. You can send the Dropify APK file directly to your friend via Bluetooth first, and then enjoy full high-speed sharing!
Will transferring files consume my mobile data plan? ▼
Never! Dropify operates entirely locally using Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth. Zero bytes of cellular or internet data are consumed, even when you transfer massive 50 GB 4K video files.
What permissions does Dropify ask for? ▼
Dropify asks for Nearby Wi-Fi Devices (or Location on Android 11 and older, as required by Android OS to scan Wi-Fi signals), Bluetooth, and Camera (only when you tap "Scan QR" to pair). Dropify does NOT request broad storage access; it uses Android's modern photo and document picker. Learn more in our Privacy Policy.
Where are received files saved on my phone? ▼
All received files are saved automatically in your device's public Downloads/Dropify folder. You can view, open, move, or share them anytime using your preferred File Manager or gallery app.
